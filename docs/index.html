Phase-Contrast Clean-Up

Phase-contrast microscopes draw a bright halo around every cell, which makes cells hard to measure automatically. This page removes the halo. Pick an image, compare before and after, and move the sliders to see the effect.

How it works
  1. Remove the halo. The image is blurred twice, once a little (detail size) and once a lot (halo size). The difference between them picks out the halo, and a share of it (strength) is subtracted.
  2. Boost local contrast. CLAHE brightens faint cell edges region by region without amplifying the flat background.
  3. Optional neural network. A U-Net looks at the result and predicts only the leftover halo, which is then subtracted. Because it removes artifacts instead of redrawing the image, cell shapes are left alone. It is too large to run in a browser, so its output here is pre-computed.

Details, training setup and caveats are in the README.
