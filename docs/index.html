1 · Halo suppression (DoG)

Blur at two scales and subtract part of the difference, flattening the bright ring around each cell. σ is a blur radius in pixels.

2 · Local contrast (CLAHE)

Stretch contrast tile by tile, capping how far any grey level can be boosted so background noise is not amplified.

    Why Classical PSNR falls below Input.

    CLAHE rewrites the tone curve to lift local contrast. PSNR compares absolute intensities with the target, so a visibly clearer image can score lower. The U-Net was trained against the target, so it keeps that scale.

    What Dice measures.

    One global Otsu threshold splits each image into cells and background. Dice is how well that cell mask overlaps the ground truth's: 1 is a perfect match, 0 is no overlap.

    Why your image gets no-reference measures.

    An upload has no clean twin, so PSNR, SSIM and Dice cannot be computed. Edge energy, background variance and foreground fraction need no target: they show whether the halo went, not fidelity.
